# Small bench follow-up for the same six scenarios

Use only the 16-fin, 0.86 mm and 16-fin, 0.60 mm specimens, each with three independently characterized end-loss settings intended to test the illustrative K=0,1,2 forms. A fixture is not assigned a measured K merely from its label. First reserve unheated hydraulic calibration points, instrument calibration, and parasitic-heat characterization; then freeze the data reduction and two distinct heated holdout pressures below and above each reviewed nominal-pressure root. Choose the holdout separation from actual instrument capability before seeing thermal outcomes; the numerical 0.0002 Pa brackets are neither instrument capability nor physical uncertainty bounds, and are not assumed experimentally resolvable. This is twelve heated holdout points across the existing six configurations, with branch 1 sealed and the measured available budget set to 0.6 times the corresponding nominal test pressure. Also reserve one unheated all-open/sealed pair per configuration at the same degraded budget, 0.6 times that configuration's reviewed nominal root pressure. Hold the measured reservoir differential identical for both members; keep these pairs separate from coefficient calibration. These six unheated pairs test branch closure without adding heated holdouts. Record the nominal all-open P operating point separately as a supply check; comparing it with sealed 0.6P cannot establish unchanged open-branch flow at fixed pressure.

| Measurement | Concrete definition and falsification use |
|---|---|
| Mechanical pressure-loss budget | Fix and document upstream/downstream reservoir or plenum stations enclosing exactly the straight channel and the chosen end-loss element once. Measure static pressure and any velocity-profile contribution needed for the mechanical-energy difference; demonstrate any neglected kinetic-head term. An arbitrary static-tap difference or unrelated station-average total pressure is not automatically the model P. Record actual P and 0.6P rather than a fan command. |
| Total flow and branch partition | Measure total mass/volume flow and enough individual branches to resolve both side channels, an open interior channel, and leakage through sealed branch 1. Compare the two side/interior conductance classes, total flow, and whether open-branch absolute flow stays unchanged in the separately reserved unheated all-open/sealed pairs at identical 0.6P. Do not use nominal all-open P versus sealed 0.6P as that test. Record actual inlet density/properties. |
| Heat into the air | Record heater voltage/current and calibrated parasitic conduction/radiation to form corrected convective heat, rather than treating electrical heater power as the air load. Independently compare inlet/outlet enthalpy rise using measured total flow and mixed-out or mass-weighted outlet temperature. Define the thermal control volume and account separately for mechanical heating inside it; do not assume all modeled P times volume flow appears in the measured channel enthalpy rise. |
| Inlet and base temperatures | Maintain and record the synthetic 25°C inlet and nominal 85°C base condition. Measure several base locations to test the uniform-base assumption, as well as inlet uniformity and outlet temperature. A base gradient or different local hot spot is evidence about the boundary/model mismatch, not a replacement definition of the synthetic uniform-base ceiling. |
| Calibration versus holdout | Use only unheated, separately reserved pressure-flow points to test whether each intended loss setting follows the fixed friction-plus-K law. If a fixed illustrative K does not describe its independently measured hydraulic behavior, report that scenario as unmatched; do not infer K from the heated holdout results. Keep all twelve heated below/above-root points out of coefficient selection and parasitic-loss fitting. Report uncertainties established by the actual instrumentation and calibration; no numerical sensor uncertainties are assumed here. |

The pressure prediction is challenged if the measured corrected heat at the predeclared below/above pressures does not cross 60 W in the predicted order or near the predicted pressure range once actual measurement uncertainty is reported. Failure of common pressure, sealed-branch leakage, the imposed inlet profile, the illustrative hydraulic law, or the uniform-base boundary separately falsifies that assumption's transfer to the fixture. Agreement would concern these two specimens and these six synthetic scenarios only; it would establish neither an aircraft requirement nor an engineering safety margin.

## Separate appendix: information needed for the full-load X-57 gap

This appendix concerns the distinct nacelle reconstruction. Passing the six 60 W component cases would not close its full-source-load discrepancy. [NACELLE_CREDIBILITY.md](../../../../../docs/NACELLE_CREDIBILITY.md) retains the mismatch at the source's 7,499 W peak / 6,080 W MCP nacelle loads, and separates equation verification, comparison with published CFD/CFD-driven ICPT outputs, and physical validation. None of those source cases is a measured or blinded thermal holdout.

| Missing information | Requirement for a meaningful next comparison or test |
|---|---|
| Independently known cooling geometry and flow split | Obtain revision-matched dimensions or metrology for the actual stator slot bank, outer-rotor/stator gap, main inlet, bypass, CMC fin passages, LV jets, wetted heat-transfer areas and contact stack. Independently establish gap/slot/bypass/LV branch flows and their mixing. The audit's mass-flow/(density×streamwise-speed) areas are hypotheses built from station means, not recovered dimensions; its fitted area/sqrt(K) cannot identify area and K separately. Do not mix earlier heat-sink revisions into the Mod II assembly. |
| Defined pressure and averaging stations | For the public-CFD comparison, obtain station geometry, static/total definitions, area/mass/flux averaging operators, velocity profiles and density treatment. Retain propeller influence already present in source external pressures, without adding it twice. For future measurements, document tap/probe locations and profile corrections on the same control volumes. A check that supplies measured or source internal downstream pressure is conditional; a predictive network test must reserve internal pressures and branch splits as outputs. |
| Actual outer-rotor motion | Establish the rotating outer-wall boundary, rotational speed, tangential/axial velocity information and mechanical work at matched operating conditions. Resolve or validate the rotating-annulus and passage/jet heat transfer appropriate to those boundaries. Pressure- or temperature-energy-equivalent speed is not a recovered swirl vector, and an inner-rotating-cylinder enhancement is not an identified outer-rotor coefficient. |
| Loss allocation and solid/contact paths | Independently characterize component electrical/mechanical losses, their spatial deposition, the winding/magnet/internal/external heat partition, and paths through interfaces, mounting, materials and contacts. Measure the relevant contact conductance and its conditions instead of adjusting it to match target temperatures. Products of published station-mean mass flow and total temperature do not provide exact integrated enthalpy or identify wall heat fractions; resolve flux weighting and rotating-wall work first. |
| Comparable thermal nodes | Match the source ICPT component definition and the air station feeding it before comparing temperatures. A hottest downstream one-dimensional winding patch is not automatically an ICPT winding node; heat-sink average, semiconductor junction, board, casing and local hot spot are distinct. For future physical validation, identify sensor locations and thermal paths that actually observe the defined comparison nodes. |
| Uncertainty and genuinely separate holdouts | Obtain instrument/calibration/repeatability information, geometry tolerances, property/loss/contact uncertainty and relevant correlations, plus numerical discretization and model-discrepancy evidence. Printed-table rounding intervals do not cover CFD error or unknown averaging. Freeze independently supported inputs or an explicit calibration subset before using new physical holdouts at matched full-load conditions; report all failed cases. The existing initial-climb inference with cruise-climb/dash checks remains retrospective source consistency, not independent experimental validation. No sensor accuracy, missing dimension, dataset or approval threshold is supplied by this plan. |

The public-source track can test a revision-matched reconstruction against the same NASA CFD stations and ICPT node definitions, while reporting which supplied pressures or fitted quantities make the comparison conditional. A future physical-validation track additionally needs actual flow, pressure, loss and node-temperature measurements with a separately reserved holdout set. Neither track is completed here; obtaining these inputs makes the discrepancy diagnosable and falsifiable, but does not guarantee it will disappear.
